Date: November 16, 2026. Fifty research agents, one angle each. Reports are shown as returned. Most figures come from secondary or vendor sources; items the agents could not check are marked unverified. General education only, not financial, legal or tax advice.

## Agent 01

**ANGLE:** Documented solo / 1-3 person businesses reaching $1M+ a year with AI (2024-2026)
**VERDICT:** WORTH WATCHING — The solo-$1M cohort is real in aggregate, but individual stories are mostly self-reported and skew toward coders and public personalities, not his profile.
**WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING:**
- Stripe aggregate data: solopreneurs earning $1M+ more than doubled 2023 to 2025; the $5M and $10M cohorts nearly tripled (verified as a Stripe figure via secondary reports; the AI link is inferred, not proven).
- The same data shows the median first-six-month revenue of solo Atlas startups fell 23% in 2025 while the top 10% rose 19%: gains are concentrated at the top (verified via secondary report).
- Named cases: Base44 (solo founder, reportedly $1.5M revenue in a month, about $80M sale to Wix; widely reported, details unverified by me); Marc Lou and Tibo Louis-Lucas (public MRR claims, self-reported, unverified). Indie Hackers admits its lists cannot be confirmed.
- Common traits of the credible cases: software products, an existing audience or heavy public building, narrow niche problems, subscription pricing, and prior skill. None were built from a $500 start with no audience, and most took 1-3 years (unverified).
**WHY IT FITS OR DOES NOT FIT HIM:** The aggregate proves the ceiling exists, but nearly every example relies on public exposure and coding or product skill, which he dislikes or lacks. His sales skill and trust are an edge only in a B2B or local-service niche.
**HOW HE WOULD MAKE MONEY:** Realistic path is a niche subscription or service sold to businesses, perhaps $50-250k a year by year 2-3; $1M is a top-decile outcome with no timeline I can verify.
**SMALLEST FIRST STEP:** Spend 3 hours reading the Stripe solopreneur report and listing 10 verified cases (with their sale channel) before building anything.
**BIGGEST RISK:** Mistaking survivorship-biased, self-reported income claims for a repeatable path.

## Agent 03

**ANGLE:** Which AI business categories got commoditized 2023-2026, how fast prices fell, and what stays valuable when AI is nearly free.
**VERDICT:** WORTH WATCHING — Selling AI itself (chatbots, writing tools, prompt packs, content sites) is a losing game; the durable money is in offers where AI is only the cost-cutting engine behind trust, licensing, local relationships or proprietary data.
**WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING:**
- GPT-4-class API cost fell from about $36 per million blended tokens (March 2023) to about $4 for GPT-4o; GPT-5 lists at $1.25 in / $10 out versus GPT-4's $30 / $60. Verified via DeepLearning.AI; some blog figures conflict. One index says the frontier has risen over the past 12 months (unverified).
- Thin "wrapper" categories (chat-with-your-docs, writing assistants, prototyping tools) were absorbed when model providers added native features; e.g. Google's Vertex RAG Engine made document chat a two-click feature. Directionally consistent, but the headline failure rates ("90% closing", "65% churn") come from low-authority blogs (unverified).
- Chatbot builders, AI receptionists, prompt packs, AI content sites: I found no hard price histories (unverified). Inference: low barrier to build means price converges to near zero.
- Survivors named: vertical workflow products with proprietary data, e.g. EvenUp (injury demand letters) (secondary source).
**WHY IT FITS OR DOES NOT FIT HIM:** Generic AI products are exactly what his AI agents can build in a weekend, so competitors can too, and he has no edge there. His real edges, sales skill, aviation domain knowledge and a stable income to fund slow bets, point to niche, trust-based offers.
**HOW HE WOULD MAKE MONEY:** AI-run services sold to a narrow professional niche with outcome guarantees, or recurring done-for-you operations for local businesses. Realistic: $0 to $3k/month at 12 months, $5-20k/month by year 3 if one niche sticks; $1M is unproven.
**SMALLEST FIRST STEP:** Spend 3 hours listing 10 niches where buyers pay for accuracy, accountability or compliance; call 5 owners (no pitch) and ask what they pay people to do today.
**BIGGEST RISK:** Building another generic AI product that a model update or a free competitor erases.
