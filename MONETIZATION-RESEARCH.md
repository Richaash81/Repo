# Monetization Research: ADR AI Autopilot + Pickler's Pulse

Date: November 15, 2026

## How this was done

- 70 research agents ran. Each took one money angle and came back with a verdict: DO NOW, DO LATER or SKIP.
- 50 covered ADR AI Autopilot. 20 covered Pickler's Pulse.
- The other 30 Pickler's Pulse agents were NOT run. The session's web search limit (200 searches) ran out. Running them without search would have produced opinions, not research.
- About 10 of the 20 Pickler's Pulse agents also ran after the limit hit. Their numbers are from memory and are marked "unverified" below.
- Most pricing figures come from vendor blogs. Treat them as directional, not exact.

## The honest headline

No agent found a way to reach $1,000,000 a year, per business, under all four of these rules at once:

1. Solo founder
2. About 33 hours a month
3. $0 new spending
4. No sales calls

Realistic year-3 ranges the agents gave under those rules:

| Business | Year 1 | Year 3 |
|---|---|---|
| ADR AI Autopilot | $0 – $25,000 | $30,000 – $150,000 |
| Pickler's Pulse | $0 – $5,000 | $5,000 – $60,000 |

$1M is still possible later. It requires breaking at least one of the four rules (people, time, money or selling).

## ADR AI Autopilot (50 agents)

### The three facts that matter most

1. **The price floor collapsed.** AI receptionists sell for $18 – $299 a month (Jobber about $29, Rosie about $49, Goodcall $59 – $199, GoHighLevel's own AI Employee $97). ADR asks $500 – $1,500. That only holds if ADR sells booked jobs and proof, not "AI answers your phone".
2. **There is zero proof on the site.** Almost every agent named this as the blocker. Nothing else works until 2 – 3 real clients and one case study exist.
3. **Founder hours are the ceiling, not cost.** Margin per client is strong (about 68% – 97%). Support is about 1 – 3 hours per client per month. That caps a solo founder at roughly 10 – 15 clients. $1M needs about 83 clients at $1,000 or 111 at $750.

### DO NOW

| # | Move | Why | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick ONE vertical and stop promoting the other 13 | Specialist agencies report higher growth, margins and fees (self-reported surveys) | $0 |
| 2 | "Founding Client" offer: free 14-day audit, then a low-cost 30-day pilot, then 50% off for 12 months, in exchange for a testimonial and case study | Every other tactic depends on proof | $0 |
| 3 | Warm network first: 20 – 30 personal messages to people already known, including fellow pilots who own or know aviation businesses | Referrals close about 3 – 4x better than cold (vendor data) | $0 |
| 4 | Check the airline's outside-business and conflict-of-interest policy and the union contract BEFORE using the pilot network | Protects the day job | $0 |
| 5 | Add an AI disclosure and recording notice to AVA's greeting, and use a one-page service agreement | Cheap legal protection | $0 |
| 6 | Self-serve missed-call revenue calculator on the vertical page, built as a GoHighLevel survey | Calculators convert about 2% – 5% of visitors to leads | $0 |
| 7 | Turn on Spanish in AVA and test it (Denver County is about 28% Hispanic) | About 1 hour of work | $0 |
| 8 | Put the demo line number everywhere: "Call AVA now" | Safe, inbound, no selling | $0 |

### Which vertical? The agents disagreed

- **Flight schools / aviation:** best credibility and warm network, but small. About 509 Part 141 schools and roughly 2,200 – 3,400 training providers in total. $1M would need about 140 schools.
- **Roofing:** highest job value of the trades compared (repair about $1,000, replacement about $9,500 – $10,000) and a Denver hail season. More crowded.
- **Recommendation:** win the first 3 proof clients in aviation, where credibility is real. Then take that proof into ONE home-service trade for volume.

### DO LATER (only after 2 – 3 paying clients)

- Referral partners at about 20% recurring for 12 months, run through GoHighLevel's affiliate manager
- Monthly "AVA Results" report and an annual prepay option to cut churn
- Commission-only setters (they do not remove the closing call)
- "Protect your Google Local Services Ads spend" hook for trades already buying ads
- "Ruby alternative" comparison page (Ruby runs about $250 – $1,725 a month)
- Trade association vendor programs (NATA associate dues $1,450 a year, PHCC $2,000 a year, so these cost money)
- Guest articles and podcasts in aviation trade media
- LinkedIn personal profile posts (about 2 hours a week, aviation only) and helpful answers in Facebook owner groups
- Manual cold email, 20 a day, never in bulk from the main domain
- Flight-school "enrollment engine" at $1,500 – $3,000 a month, once enrollments can be proven

### SKIP

| Angle | Reason |
|---|---|
| AI voice agent making cold outbound calls | FCC ruled AI voices fall under the TCPA. Consent is required. $500 – $1,500 per call exposure |
| Dental and med spa | HIPAA. GoHighLevel's HIPAA add-on is reported at about $297 a month (unverified). Funded competitors integrate with practice software |
| Veterinary | Crowded at $49 – $299 a month |
| Aircraft maintenance shops | Short on capacity, not on calls. Annuals booked 12 months out |
| GoHighLevel SaaS mode, snapshots, white-label wholesale | Needs the $497 a month plan or competes with funded platforms |
| Review automation as a standalone product | Commodity. Keep it as a bundled add-on |
| Hundreds of city-by-industry SEO pages | Google's scaled-content policy. 5 – 10 hand-built pages at most |
| Drone services brokerage | Low ticket, liability, existing pilot networks |
| Franchise and multi-location deals | Enterprise sales cycle with no references |
| Course or community on building AI agencies | No results to teach from yet, and the FTC is actively pursuing AI business-opportunity sellers |
| Gumroad prompt packs | Median product earns about $88 in its lifetime |
| Paid networking groups | BNI about $1,500 – $2,000 a year all-in. C12 about $13,000+ a year |

### One honesty flag on the current site

The home page cites "62% of calls to small businesses are never answered". CallRail's 2025 data on 1.1 million conversations puts the home-services miss rate near 14%. Lead with the conservative number, or show both with sources.

## Pickler's Pulse (20 agents ran, 30 did not)

### The three facts that matter most

1. **Traffic is the whole game.** Amazon pays about 3% on sports gear, which works out to roughly 2 cents per visitor. $10,000 a month from Amazon alone would need about 460,000 visitors a month.
2. **Pure affiliate is the thinnest layer.** The biggest pickleball sites add other layers. Pickleheads (about $3.9M raised, 600,000 monthly visitors claimed) earns from event and lesson fees plus ads. Pickleball Effect sells its own products.
3. **The legality tracker is the unique asset.** Nobody else maintains it. It is the best way in for search, for AI citations and for email signups.

### DO NOW

| # | Move | Why | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Finish scoring. Only 27 of 106 lines have a Pulse Score | Every other tactic lands on these pages | $0 |
| 2 | Apply to direct brand affiliate programs. Suggested first five: Selkirk (AvantLink), JOOLA (Awin), JustPaddles, Phantom, CRBN | Pays better than Amazon. Rates found were conflicting, so confirm each one | $0 |
| 3 | Make Pickleball Central the default buy button ("up to 10%" on its own page, 30-day cookie). Keep Amazon second | Higher rate, longer cookie | $0 |
| 4 | One "Verified Paddle Discount Codes" page, plus a code box on each paddle page, with a "date last tested" line | Competitors already prove demand. "Verified" is the edge | $0 |
| 5 | Switch Amazon search links to direct product links | New Associates accounts can be closed without 3 sales in 180 days | $0 |
| 6 | Protect the aggregator model: quote one short sentence per source, always link, no reviewer images, publish a correction and opt-out email, add something first-hand to each page | A Google quality demotion or reviewer complaint would wipe out everything | $0 |
| 7 | "Find my paddle" quiz ending in 3 scored picks and an email capture | Higher intent than a ranking list (benchmarks unverified) | $0 |
| 8 | Put a "weekly legality and price alerts" signup on the rankings and legality pages | Builds a channel that does not depend on Google | $0 |

### DO LATER

- Display ads once traffic exists (Raptive needs 25,000 pageviews a month; at about $20 per 1,000 views, $10,000 a month needs about 500,000 pageviews)
- Weekly newsletter sponsors (about $1 – $5 per subscriber per year; flat $75 – $200 per issue under 3,000 subscribers)
- Clearly labeled sponsor slots with a written independence policy. Never next to a sponsor's own score
- A small hand-checked set of "Paddle A vs Paddle B" pages (15 – 20, not thousands)
- Shoes as the next scored category
- Facebook group routine: three membership questions including an optional email, plus three scheduled posts a week
- Pinterest, only after scoring is finished

### SKIP

| Angle | Reason |
|---|---|
| Amazon as the main engine | About 3% commission and recent rate cuts |
| Automated price-drop emails with Amazon links | Amazon's rules on prices and on affiliate links in email. Use a hand-updated "Deal Watch" page instead |
| Paid membership | Needs about 200,000 free subscribers to earn $10,000 a month |
| Facebook page payouts from Meta | Negligible for a niche page |
| Faceless YouTube score videos | Templated videos are the pattern YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy targets (unverified) |

### Not researched (search limit) — worth a second run

These are the angles most likely to matter for a $1M path, because they add layers on top of affiliate income:

- Licensing a "Pulse Score" badge or annual awards to brands and retailers
- Selling sentiment and score data reports to paddle makers
- Legality alerts as a service for tournament directors and clubs
- Portfolio math across Pickler's Pulse, Bat Pulse, Racquet Call and new sports (golf, padel)
- High-ticket affiliates: ball machines, court builders, camps and travel
- Black Friday and holiday plan
- Player ratings ("Players' Pulse") as a long-term moat
- Exit value of content sites
- Email list revenue per subscriber, giveaways, merch, used-paddle and demo programs, senior-focused products
